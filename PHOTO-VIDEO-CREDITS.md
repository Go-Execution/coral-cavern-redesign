# Coral Cavern client design mockup

Presentation-only concept: seven static pages, original visual design, genuine photography, subtle ambient motion and responsive layouts. No payment gateway, order processing, inquiry sending, inventory synchronization or production commerce integrations are configured.

- Home, Shop, Services, Learn, About, Contact and specimen detail concepts.
- Search, filters, saves and bag use browser-local demonstration data.
- Checkout and quote form submissions are simulated only.
- Catalogue: 26 reference listings, 21 available real livestock photographs. Five uncertain images omitted.
- Product prices, availability, specimen identity, ownership and review permissions require client confirmation before commercial release.
- The original site's direct access was blocked by its verification screen. Public indexed content and the supplied reference were used. The supplied reference's original product photographs are locally stored, not hotlinked.
- Reviews are short excerpts from the supplied reference, not independently verified Google reviews. No ratings or invented reviews added.
- Existing written shipping terms have conflicting deadlines; the mockup links to current terms and does not promise invented coverage.
